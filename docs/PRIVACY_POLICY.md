# Twelve Pits (Ayo): Privacy Policy

> **Note for the owner:** Phase 9 will update this policy. The two-phone mode will use Bluetooth and Wi-Fi to talk to the other phone, so this text must be reviewed and changed before that version is released.

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Twelve Pits (Ayo) is a board game published by Neribo Group ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the game does with information. The short version: **it collects nothing.**

## What we collect

Nothing. The game has:

- no account and no sign-in,
- no advertising,
- no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools,
- no way to connect to the internet. The app does not ask for the internet permission.

We do not collect, receive, sell or share any personal information.

## What stays on your phone

The game keeps a small amount of data **only on your phone**, so that it can work the way you expect:

- your settings (music, music volume, sound effects, vibration, seed speed),
- the player names and avatars you choose on the game setup screen,
- the games won in each mode, and who starts the next game,
- the game you are playing, so you can continue it later.

This data never leaves your phone. It is not backed up to the cloud by the app. You can clear it at any time by choosing Reset scores in Settings, or by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game in your phone's settings.

## Permissions

The game asks for one permission, **Vibrate**. It lets the phone vibrate during play when you switch Vibration on in Settings. Android does not show a question for this permission, and the game never uses it when Vibration is off.

## Children

The game does not collect any information from anyone, including children. It has no chat, no links to other sites and no purchases.

## Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date before the new version is released.
